# Sales Performance Power BI Dashboard

##  Project Overview
The **Sales Performance Dashboard** provides an in-depth analysis of sales, profit, and quantity metrics across multiple time periods, products, and promotions. This Power BI report helps business users monitor top and bottom-performing products, track trends over time, and measure the impact of promotions on sales and profitability.

### 2. Sales, Profit, and Quantity Comparison
Dynamic visualizations compare **Total Sales**, **Total Profit**, and **Units Sold** with interactive date slicers.

- Total Sales: 121M

---

### 3. Date Range Analysis
Multiple **date filters** allow users to view performance across specific time periods (e.g., 2020-2023), helping identify seasonal patterns or growth trends.

---

### 4. Customer and Product-Level Details
The data table section provides transaction-level details with the following columns:
- Customer ID, Name, Order ID, Product ID, Product Name  
- Date, Discount, Discount Percentage  
- Net Sales, Price per Unit, Profit  
- Promotion ID, Promotion Name, Total Sales, Units Sold  

Interactive filters for **Date**, **Customer Name**, **Product Name**, and **Promotion Name** are available.

## Business Insights
- Apple products dominate both sales and profit.  
- Lower-priced FMCG products (like soaps and lotions) contribute minimally.  
- Promotions like "Weekend Flash Sale" yield the largest average discounts.  
- Sales peaks correspond to major promotional periods.  
- Profit trends strongly correlate with net sales, indicating steady margins.
